Foss PDF is a simple, privacy-friendly open-source PDF reader for Android.

Features:
- Open PDFs via the system file picker, with a list of recently opened files
- Page through documents with swipe gestures
- Double-tap to zoom a page, tap to hide/show the controls
- Full-text search with hit counter, previous/next and highlighting in the document
- Share PDFs via the Android share sheet
- Rename PDFs directly

No permissions required, no network access, no tracking, no ads.
Fully open source (GPL-3.0).
